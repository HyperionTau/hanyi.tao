About

I am an applied economist working on international trade, macroeconomics, and firm innovation, with a focus on the Chinese economy. My research combines structural modeling with China's large policy experiments — targeted subsidies, tariffs, and place-based programs — to understand how firms innovate, allocate capital, and respond to trade shocks. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and have been an Assistant Professor at ShanghaiTech University since 2019.
